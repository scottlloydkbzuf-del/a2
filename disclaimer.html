2. Natural Fiber Disclosures and Care Notices

Our bespoke garments are fashioned from genuine, unadulterated animal fibers including virgin sheep wool, cashmere, mohair, and camel hair. Although natural wool possesses exceptional breathability and moisture-wicking properties, it requires specialized care, breathable wooden hanger storage, and professional gentle dry cleaning. Herringbone Fit is not liable for garment shrinkage, fiber felting, or moth damage resulting from client failure to maintain proper wool storage conditions.

Because natural fleece fibers are dyed in artisanal mill batches, slight shade variations may occur between seasonal fabric runs. Herringbone Fit ensures that all components of an individual suit are cut from the identical bolt of cloth to guarantee absolute chromatic uniformity; however, matching replacement trousers ordered years later to existing jackets may exhibit subtle tonal distinctions due to natural fabric aging and ultraviolet light exposure.
