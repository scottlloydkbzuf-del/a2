● The Mercer Street Atelier

Preserving the Art of the Hand-Cut Suit.

Trained in the historic tailoring houses of London's Mayfair and established in Manhattan's cast-iron architectural district, Herringbone Fit merges centuries of English bespoke discipline with refined contemporary elegance.

Pure Sourcing

The Integrity of Natural Wool

We work exclusively with certified historic textile mills across Scotland, Northern England, and Biella, Italy. Every fleece is ethically shorn, washed in mineral-rich river waters, and spun with natural crimp intact.

When woven in the signature herringbone pattern, these wools exhibit unmatched tensile resilience, holding their sharp tailored creases through years of rigorous urban movement.
