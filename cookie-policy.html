Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Herringbone Fit Atelier • 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Understanding Web Cookies and Local Storage

This Cookie Policy explains how Herringbone Fit deploys browser cookies, web beacons, and related storage mechanisms to deliver a seamless, intuitive, and secure experience across our digital atelier. A cookie is a compact text file stored on your device when you load web pages, enabling our systems to remember your interface preferences and session states between visits. We employ these technologies responsibly to enhance booking navigation and analyze aggregated traffic without compromising client privacy.

Cookies play a vital role in ensuring that our interactive consultation calendar functions efficiently, preventing fraudulent automated bookings, and preserving your visual viewing preferences across page transitions. By continuing to browse our website or submitting fitting consultation requests, you consent to the placement and retrieval of cookies in accordance with the stipulations detailed in this policy. You can adjust your cookie settings at any time.

Our digital reservation platform never deploys tracking technologies to intercept private financial credentials or extract personal contact information without your prior knowledge and explicit permission. All cookie-derived telemetry is processed under rigorous military-grade encryption standards, ensuring that your digital interactions with our tailoring atelier remain completely confidential and permanently shielded from unauthorized commercial marketing brokers or third-party data aggregators across all global networks.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed on Our Portal

Our digital infrastructure utilizes strictly necessary cookies essential for core website functionality, security authentication, and form processing. Without these essential cookies, our appointment booking engine cannot preserve your selected consultation date or securely process reservation submissions between consecutive page views. Because these cookies are indispensable to service delivery, they cannot be deactivated within our system settings without severely degrading site performance.

We also implement performance and analytical cookies, including the Google Analytics tracking tag G-0LY0HY7L01, to gather anonymous statistical telemetry regarding visitor engagement and reading habits across our sartorial journal. These analytical cookies record non-identifying data such as page load speeds, reading durations, and navigation paths, helping our development team optimize server responsiveness and enhance mobile layout clarity. Analytical data is evaluated strictly in aggregate form, preserving client anonymity.

3. Managing Preferences and Browser Settings

Most modern internet web browsers automatically accept cookies by default; however, users retain complete technical freedom to adjust their browser security preferences to block or purge cookies entirely. Through your browser's settings menu, you can decline third-party analytical cookies, configure notifications whenever a new cookie is placed, or erase all stored cache files upon concluding your browsing session. Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent our fitting calendar from functioning properly.

For step-by-step guidance on modifying cookie preferences across widely used web browsers such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, and Microsoft Edge, consult your browser's official support documentation. Furthermore, visitors may opt out of standardized analytical tracking across the web by installing the official Google Analytics opt-out browser extension. We respect your technical autonomy and ensure our sartorial treatises remain accessible regardless of your chosen tracking settings.

4. Institutional Governance and Contact Information

Herringbone Fit reserves the right to update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological upgrades, changes to our online booking architecture, or evolving digital privacy standards. Any substantive amendments will be posted immediately to this page along with a revised effective date at the top of the policy. We encourage frequent visitors and prospective clients to review this document periodically to remain informed about our technical data practices.

Should you have any questions regarding our deployment of cookies, or if you require technical support managing your browser preferences while booking bespoke tailoring consultations, our administrative staff is pleased to assist. Please direct your inquiries to Herringbone Fit, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our client desk at +1-888-777-5845. We remain dedicated to upholding absolute transparency across all digital channels.
