## 2. Layout & Design Fingerprint
- **Color Palette:**

  - Primary Tone: British Racing Green (`#1c3d2e`)
  - Accent Tone: Cognac Saddle Leather & Amber Gold (`#a16207` / `#ca8a04`)
  - Typography: Chalk Charcoal (`#18181b`) and Slate Grey (`#475569`)
- **Typography:**
  - Display Serif: `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif`
  - Body Clean Sans: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif`
  - Monospace Numbers: `'JetBrains Mono', monospace`
- **Unique Layout Identity:**
  - Editorial Savile Row layout with asymmetrical hero, bespoke monogram crest (`HF`), 4-step fitting journey cards, and interactive consultation forms.
  - Distinctly different from previous dark nocturnal ramen designs.

## 3. Compliance & Standards
- **HTML Only:** 100% static HTML. Zero PHP.
- **Blogs:** Exactly 3 long-form treatises in `blog/*.html` (1,400+ words each) with tables, FAQs, author bios, and schema.
- **Policy Pages:** Strictly 5-6 lines per paragraph (~65 to 95 words) across all 4 policies.
