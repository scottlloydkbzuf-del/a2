2. Fitting Appointments and Commission Policies

Because our bespoke garments require upwards of sixty hours of individualized bench tailoring, fitting consultations are allocated on an exclusive, timed basis. Patrons are requested to arrive punctually at their designated appointment time to ensure our master cutter can devote full attention to your posture analysis and baste evaluations. In the event you must reschedule a fitting, we require at least forty-eight hours advance notice to reallocate your reserved salon hour to another patron.

Bespoke garments involve significant upfront fabric procurement and labor allocation once cutting commences. Deposits submitted upon pattern drafting and cloth cutting are non-refundable once shears strike the wool cloth. Our atelier commits to executing up to three separate fitting stages to achieve structural balance; however, requests for drastic silhouette revisions differing from the originally commissioned style will be treated as separate bespoke orders subject to additional fees.

All bespoke garment commissions remain strictly subject to authentic European textile mill availability and seasonal weaving schedules. While we make every endeavor to secure chosen cloth swatches promptly from foreign suppliers, international shipping delays from Scottish Highlands and Northern Italian mills may occasionally adjust garment delivery timelines. We communicate all supply chain updates immediately and provide equivalent luxury cloth alternatives should a specific seasonal weave become discontinued by the foreign weaving mill.
