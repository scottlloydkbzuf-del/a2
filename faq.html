Trimmings & Accents

Horn Buttons & Silk Gimp

Every detail on a Herringbone Fit garment reflects artisanal dedication. Our buttons are carved from ethically sourced water buffalo horn and finished with subtle matte polishing.

Buttonholes are cut and bound entirely by hand with heavy silk gimp thread, taking up to forty-five minutes per lapel hole to achieve structural perfection.

Our bespoke two-piece suits start at $2,850 in essential British worsted wools, scaling upward based on rare cloth selections such as 100% Escorial wool or pure vicuña blends. All prices include multiple fittings and pattern preservation.

First-time clients require three fittings: the skeleton baste fitting, the forward fitting with sleeves and canvas basted, and the final delivery fitting. Once your master paper pattern is refined and stored, future commissions require only one fitting.
