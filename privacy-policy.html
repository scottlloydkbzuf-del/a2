Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Herringbone Fit Atelier • 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Scope and Institutional Commitment

Herringbone Fit operates this digital atelier and tailoring publication to provide prospective patrons with streamlined access to bespoke fitting consultations and educational craftsmanship archives. This Privacy Policy details the systematic protocols we implement to collect, secure, and manage client records when you explore our digital portfolio or schedule private fittings at our Mercer Street atelier. We are strictly committed to preserving confidentiality and ensuring total transparency regarding personal data handling across all our digital touchpoints.

When visitors browse our sartorial library or request appointment allocations, our technical servers record encrypted access data necessary to preserve site performance and protect online communications. We manage all client details entrusted to our tailoring staff with profound institutional care, ensuring that unauthorized third parties never access your body measurements, appointment schedules, or garment commission archives. By using our website or booking bespoke tailoring services, you acknowledge and accept the comprehensive guidelines defined throughout this document.

2. Information We Collect and Process

To properly schedule fitting appointments and coordinate bespoke garment commissions, Herringbone Fit collects essential contact information including your full legal name, telephone number, electronic mail address, and specific sartorial preferences. This data allows our concierge to confirm private appointments, provide fitting updates, and ensure our cutting staff prepares suitable fabric swatches prior to your arrival at our salon. We obtain this information solely through your voluntary input during appointment scheduling.

In addition to self-submitted booking details, our digital servers automatically log non-identifying telemetry including browser classification, device operating system, referring URLs, and network session durations. This diagnostic telemetry is analyzed exclusively in the aggregate to identify server latency, prevent automated reservation fraud, and refine our typography across mobile platforms. We maintain an impermeable boundary between technical session telemetry and individual client garment records, safeguarding patron anonymity.

3. Data Retention and Institutional Security

Personal contact records and fitting notes submitted through our reservation portal are retained only as long as necessary to complete your garment commissions and fulfill legal taxation obligations. Following the final delivery and press of your bespoke suit, client measurements are archived in an encrypted repository to facilitate future commissions without requiring repeated anatomical evaluations. We never store credit card credentials on local servers, routing all transactions through certified third-party payment gateways.

We maintain stringent administrative, physical, and technological security barriers designed to guard against unauthorized access, data compromise, or accidental disclosure of client records. All digital transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security standards. Our tailoring atelier staff receives rigorous training regarding client confidentiality, guaranteeing that private garment files remain accessible solely to authorized cutting personnel managing your commissions.

4. Your Rights and Contact Protocols

Patrons retain absolute rights under prevailing global privacy regulations to review, modify, or demand the permanent deletion of their archived client profiles stored within our reservation systems. If you wish to update your contact parameters, unsubscribe from seasonal cloth arrival announcements, or request a complete summary of your stored measurements, our privacy officer will process your request promptly. We do not charge administrative fees for legitimate data verification requests submitted through verified communication channels.

Should you have any inquiries or concerns regarding the stewardship of your information under this Privacy Policy, please contact our administrative offices directly. You may reach our management team by writing to Herringbone Fit, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by calling our telephone desk at +1-888-777-5845 during normal atelier hours. We remain committed to resolving all patron privacy inquiries with absolute discretion, professional integrity, and timely correspondence.
